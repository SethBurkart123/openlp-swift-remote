# Privacy policy

OpenLP Swift Remote connects to the OpenLP server you choose. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated accounts. The developer does not receive your service content or connection details through the app.

The app stores connection preferences, your optional username, and the live-controls preference on your device. Passwords are held in memory for the current app session. Service content and media previews are fetched from your configured server to display in the app. Connection and authentication requests are sent to that server; its operator controls any server-side logging. HTTP connections are not encrypted; HTTPS can be enabled when supported by your server.

Apple may collect TestFlight diagnostics and feedback under Apple's policies. If you send feedback by email or through GitHub, that service processes the information you choose to share, and the developer uses it to respond and improve the app. Avoid including passwords or private service content in feedback.
